Customer Retention & Revenue Optimization Analysis

Business Objective
This project explores an online retail dataset to understand what drives revenue, how customers behave over time, and where the business is losing value through churn.

The goal was simple:
Identify where revenue comes from, where retention drops, and what levers can realistically improve growth.

Dataset

Online retail transaction data containing:
Invoice numbers
Customer IDs
Purchase dates
Revenue values

The dataset allows customer-level analysis across time.

Tools & Methods Used

Python (Pandas, NumPy) for data cleaning and aggregation

RFM segmentation to identify customer value tiers

Cohort analysis to measure retention behavior over time

Churn rate calculation at segment level

Customer Lifetime Value estimation

Revenue growth simulation scenarios

Tableau for interactive dashboard visualization

Key Insights

A small portion of customers (~26%) generate nearly 80% of total revenue, confirming a strong Pareto pattern.
First-month retention is low (~20%), indicating early drop-off. Overall churn rate is approximately 33%, which signals retention improvement opportunities.
Loyal customers contribute the majority of revenue and show significantly lower churn.
A 10% increase in Average Order Value (AOV) could drive an estimated ~$1M revenue uplift.

Strategic Recommendations

Prioritize retention of high-value loyal customers through targeted engagement.
Improve onboarding and early engagement to reduce Month 1 churn.
Increase AOV using bundling, pricing optimization, and cross-selling strategies.
Develop targeted campaigns to reactivate churned customers.

Business Impact

This analysis highlights revenue concentration risk, early retention leakage, and measurable growth opportunities.
By focusing on high-value segments and improving early retention, the business can significantly increase revenue without relying solely on new customer acquisition.
