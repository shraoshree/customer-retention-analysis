Customer Retention & Revenue Optimization Analysis
Business Objective

Analyze online retail transaction data to understand revenue drivers, retention behavior, churn rate, and growth opportunities.

Dataset

Online retail transaction dataset containing invoices, customer IDs, purchase dates, and revenue.

Tools & Techniques

Python (Pandas, NumPy)

RFM Segmentation

Cohort Retention Analysis

Churn Rate Modeling

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Revenue Growth Simulation

Tableau (Dashboard Visualization)

Key Findings

26% of customers generate 80% of revenue (Pareto effect)

Month 1 retention ≈ 20%

Churn rate ≈ 33%

Loyal customers contribute majority of revenue

Increasing AOV by 10% increases revenue by ~$1M

Strategic Recommendations

Focus marketing on high-value loyal customers

Improve early retention (Month 1)

Optimize AOV through bundling and pricing strategy

Reactivate churned customers with targeted campaigns

Business Impact

This analysis identifies revenue concentration, retention leakage, and quantifies growth opportunities using data-driven simulation.
