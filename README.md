Customer Retention & Revenue Optimization Analysis
Business Objective

This project explores an online retail dataset to understand what drives revenue, how customers behave over time, and where the business is losing value through churn.

The goal was simple: identify where revenue comes from, where retention drops, and what levers can realistically improve growth.

Dataset

Online retail transaction data containing:

Invoice numbers

Customer IDs

Purchase dates

Revenue values

The dataset enables customer-level analysis across time.

Tools & Methods Used

Python (Pandas, NumPy) for data cleaning and aggregation

RFM segmentation to identify customer value tiers

Cohort analysis to measure retention behavior over time

Churn rate calculation at the segment level

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) estimation

Revenue growth simulation scenarios

Tableau for interactive dashboard visualization

Key Insights

Approximately 26% of customers generate nearly 80% of total revenue, confirming a strong Pareto pattern.

First-month retention is low (~20%), indicating early drop-off.

Overall churn rate is approximately 33%, highlighting retention improvement opportunities.

Loyal customers contribute the majority of revenue and exhibit significantly lower churn.

A 10% increase in Average Order Value (AOV) could drive an estimated ~$1M revenue uplift.

Strategic Recommendations

Focus marketing and retention efforts on high-value loyal customers.

Improve early engagement to reduce Month 1 churn.

Increase AOV through bundling, pricing optimization, and cross-selling strategies.

Re-engage churned customers using targeted campaigns.

Business Impact

This analysis identifies revenue concentration risk, retention leakage in early lifecycle stages, and measurable growth opportunities.

By improving early retention and increasing AOV, the business can drive significant revenue growth without relying solely on new customer acquisition.
